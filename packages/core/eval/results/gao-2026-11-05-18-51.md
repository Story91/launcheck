## Misses and false alarms

### B-422670 (positive)

GAO: Pre-award challenge to a facilities-maintenance RFQ that automatically penalized prior-experience examples not covering multiple facilities under one contract at least 200 miles apart; GAO sustained because GSA could not justify these experience criteria (the two-phase scheme challenge was denied).

Missed: S4_EXPERIENCE_LOCK. Label note: Experience criteria shaped so that only a vendor with a particular single-contract, geographically dispersed portfolio scores well; closest fit is the experience-lock signal (not tied to a named incumbent, so S4 is an approximation).

### B-414987 (positive)

GAO: Pre-award challenge to a new 5,000-gallon maximum tank capacity in a call-when-needed airtanker RFP that excluded very large airtankers; GAO sustained because the record did not show the cap was reasonably necessary to meet the agency's needs.

Missed: S3_OVER_SPECIFICATION. Label note: A maximum-capacity cap that excludes larger aircraft is a technical limit beyond the agency's demonstrated needs (FAR 11.002), i.e., over-specification.

### B-413876.2 (positive)

GAO: Pre-award challenge to folder/inserter specifications (built around the PS200 or equivalent) requiring load-on-the-fly high-capacity feeders; GAO sustained because the IRS did not show that capability was necessary when alternating feeders could meet the same need.

Missed: S3_OVER_SPECIFICATION. Label note: Feature-level specifications that exceeded the demonstrated need; GAO also notes the CLINs reference the brand-name PS200, but that is paraphrase, so S2 is not claimed from the excerpts.

### B-408196 (positive)

GAO: Pre-award challenge to an FSS RFQ limited to the AtHoc mass-notification brand and authorized AtHoc resellers; GAO sustained because the limited-source justification did not define the needs, explain why AtHoc was essential, or reflect adequate market research.

Missed: S2_BRAND_LOCK. Label note: The quoted requirement names a single proprietary product (AtHoc) with no 'or equal'; the other two excerpts are routine amendment text included to reach length.

### B-416733 (positive)

GAO: Pre-award challenge to an FSS audit-support RFQ requiring vendors' schedule labor categories to 'align precisely' with PWS minimum years of experience, so 'minimum of 10 years' could not satisfy a 12-year position; GAO sustained the provision as unduly restrictive.

Missed: S3_OVER_SPECIFICATION. Label note: An evaluation rule stricter than the agency's need (form-matching of schedule text rather than capability); arguably S4 as well, but it is not tied to an incumbent or product, so S3 is the primary label.

### B-424321.2 (negative)

GAO: Post-award protest of the technical evaluation (missing strengths, disparate treatment), price realism and the best-value tradeoff for REMIS support; GAO denied because the evaluation was reasonable and the FOPR did not provide for price realism.

Unexpected S2_BRAND_LOCK: The notice says to maintain operation of the REMIS system and has no equivalence clause; S2_BRAND_LOCK applies.
